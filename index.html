How a match goes

Host a room

Enter your name and create a room. You get a short code to send to one friend.

Friend joins

They paste the code on their own phone or laptop and wait for you to launch.

Chase the signal

Move with WASD or the arrows. First runner to the pulse scores it, and it jumps somewhere new.

Clock hits zero

Most signals wins. Hit play again and the room is ready for a rematch.
