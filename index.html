How to play

No install, no account. One friend hosts, the other joins with a code, and the match launches when both runners are ready.

1

Host a room

Enter your name and create a room. You get a short room code to share with one friend.

2

Join from anywhere

Your opponent pastes the code on their own device and waits for the launch.

3

Chase the signal

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. First runner to the pulse scores it.

4

Beat the clock

Most signals collected when the 60-second timer hits zero wins. Hit play again to rematch.

Why it works

Simple rules, shared state, and instant feedback make every match easy to start and fun to replay.
